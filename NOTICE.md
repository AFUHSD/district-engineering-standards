# Notice

## What is original

The documents, the ruleset JSON files, the custom-property schema and the PowerShell scripts in this repository
were written by Agua Fria Union High School District and are licensed under the MIT license in
[`LICENSE`](LICENSE). To the best of our knowledge no file was copied from another project. Some rule names and
short phrases follow Peninsula's own published wording, and are credited below.

## What is credited

The ideas come from Peninsula School District (PSD401), and are credited as ideas:

- tiering repositories and targeting organization rulesets by a custom property;
- starting rulesets in evaluate and flipping them to active one at a time;
- a canary repository that breaks on purpose;
- an issue contract with a definition of done that an agent can read;
- the `/plan` and `/lfg` loop and its definition-of-done gate.

`docs/plugin-on-windows.md` describes how to use their `psd-coding-system` plugin. It quotes command names and
configuration keys from that plugin so a reader can follow along. It includes none of the plugin's code. The plugin
is published by Peninsula under its own license at <https://github.com/psd401/psd-claude-plugins>.

The format of `docs/why-this-setup.md`, a numbered table of problems that links to a short answer for each, is
adapted from Byungkyu Lee's `claude-academic-setup`, published under the MIT license at
<https://github.com/letitbk/claude-academic-setup>. None of its text is reproduced.

## If we got this wrong

If you believe something here reproduces your work, open an issue and it will be corrected or removed.
